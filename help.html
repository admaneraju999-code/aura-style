Scanning garments

Use the Scan New Item button to photograph a garment. Aura's built-in Computer Vision automatically detects the clothing item, removes the background, and classifies it as a top, bottom, or shoe — extracting its color and pattern.

Building outfits

Open the Outfit Builder, drag items onto the canvas, or press Magic Mix to get an AI-suggested pairing. Review the Synergy scores and save your favorite looks.

Understanding AI scores

Color Palette measures how well the colors of your pieces harmonize. Occasion Match estimates how suitable the look is for a given event type. Both are calculated by the Computer Vision / ML backend.
